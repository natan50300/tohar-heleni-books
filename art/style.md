```
STYLE: Soft, warm children's picture-book illustration, hand-painted gouache and colored-pencil texture. Muted dusty pastel palette suited to bedtime: gentle, low contrast, no bright white, no neon. Rounded shapes, soft edges, simple cozy backgrounds, warm glowing light. Toddler proportions: big heads, short limbs, chubby cheeks. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. Keep the bottom quarter of the image calm and uncluttered (plain floor, grass, blanket or sky) because text will be placed over it; keep faces and important details in the upper three quarters. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, signs or watermarks anywhere in the image. Use the attached character sheets as the exact reference for each girl's face, hair, skin tone and body proportions; only the clothes change.
```
